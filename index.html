The Villa

La Selva Giardino del Belvedere

A private stone farmhouse on the Chianti hills surrounded by centuries-old oaks, olive groves and meadows. 50km from Florence (1 hr), 45km from Siena, 40km from Arezzo. Private salt-water pool, 2 wood-fired pizza ovens, draft beer + Prosecco fridge by the pool, barbecue, 2 fully equipped kitchens, 2 washing machines, Wi-Fi, satellite TV, piano, wood fireplaces. Linens, pool towels, weekly cleaning, final clean and tourist tax all included. Nearest village Montegonzi (1.5km). Grocery stop recommended in Montevarchi (6km) on arrival — COOP supermarket open Mon–Sat 8am–8:30pm and Il Mercatale organic farmer's market open Mon–Sat 9–1pm & 4–8:30pm.

🌿 The rhythm: Every day has a slow moment — either a relaxed morning or a villa afternoon. You're never racing out the door. Wednesday is deliberately left open so the group decides on the day.

🗺️ Choose your Wednesday:
Option A · Chianti Scenic Drive
Chiantigiana SR222 → Greve → Radda in Chianti
Drive the most beautiful road in Tuscany through vineyard-draped hills and cypress-lined ridges. Stop in Greve in Chianti — arcaded triangular piazza, browse the market. Visit Enoteca del Gallo Nero for wine tasting (the best wine shop in Chianti Classico — taste your way through local producers and buy bottles to take home or to Austria). Continue to Radda in Chianti for a late Aperol Spritz on the terrace before heading back for the winery dinner. Relaxed, scenic, very Chianti.
Option B · Medieval City
Siena — 45km, 50 min from villa
One of Italy's most beautiful medieval cities, far less crowded than Florence. The Piazza del Campo — the shell-shaped main square — is one of the world's great public spaces. The striped black and white marble Duomo is extraordinary. Walk the medieval streets, stop for an Aperol Spritz on the Campo. Compact and walkable — 3 hours is plenty. Back by 5pm for the winery dinner.
